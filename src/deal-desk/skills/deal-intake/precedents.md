# Precedent memo

> Illustrative planning record; decisions are internal to this Deal System and do not submit a customer order or external offer.

## P-2025-07 · Northwind Pharmacy · thermometer line extension (2025)

- **Ask:** 18-month price protection on the THM range, 11 % rebate, Net-75. GM at the ask was 30.9 %, below the floor.
- **Decision (Dana, VP Finance):** 12-month protection **with a CPI cap** for the rest of the term, 10 % rebate, Net-60. Final GM 33.2 %.
- **Why:** an 18-month freeze overlapped a forecast component increase, so GM fell below the floor. The CPI cap gave Northwind the price certainty it asked for without the open-ended cost exposure.
- **Learning:** Northwind's procurement ranks **price certainty first, rebate second**. They accepted the CPI cap within 2 days.

## P-2024-11 · Contoso Health Mart (regional) · MDF

- An analyst treated a $250K MDF as opex and showed GM 33.4 %. Restated as contra-revenue, GM was 31.6 %, below the floor. Re-cut as sell-through MDF at $150K, approved within authority.
- **Learning:** MDF is always contra-revenue.

## P-2025-03 · Fabrikam Drug Co. · 24-month protection

- Approved with 24-month protection by mistake. The model ignored the year-2 sensor cost increase. Realized GM was 29.8 % against 32.6 % at approval. This triggered the requirement to price all multi-year deals against the component cost forecast.
